AMD Pre-Alert Automation

Turned a seven-step manual email task into a four-step form, so the team spends less time on emails and a backup can take over at any point.

78%faster per pre-alert
~70 minsaved every day
0.19 → 0.04VFTE workload
Built one master list of receivers, so email addresses are updated in one place
Automated document saving and email sending with the right recipients and subject
Added checks for missing fields and duplicate sending, plus a job aid for new staff
Read the full case study

The problem

  • Around 40 pre-alerts a day, up to 170 in peak season
  • Over 10 email lists to remember across 5 situations
  • Recipients, subject and attachment all done by hand
  • No record of what was sent, so a backup could not continue

Other results

  • Process steps cut from 7 to 4
  • Manual error points cut from 6 to 3
  • Backup handover cut from 15 minutes to none
  • Learning time cut from a full day to about 15 minutes
