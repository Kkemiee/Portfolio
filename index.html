AMD Pre-Alert Automation

Turned a seven-step manual email task into a four-step form, cutting handling time by 78% and making it easy for a backup to take over at any point.

The problem

Every AMD shipment needs a pre-alert email with its shipping documents before customs clearance. The agent sends around 40 a day, and up to 170 in peak season.

  • Over 10 email lists to remember across 5 different situations
  • Recipients, subject and attachment all done by hand
  • No record of what was sent, so a backup could not tell where to continue

What I built

  • A master receiver list, so each email address is updated in one place
  • A flow that saves incoming shipping documents automatically
  • A short form where the agent picks the document, the receiver and the AWB number
  • A flow that sends the pre-alert with the right recipients, CC and subject, then marks the file as sent
  • Checks for missing fields and duplicate sending, plus error alerts

Claim Review Automation

Connected the full claim journey, from the agent’s submission to the customer’s settlement letter, into one tracked process.

The problem

When a shipment is lost or damaged, the claim passes from agent to manager to team leader. Every step was handled by hand through emails and documents.

  • No single place to see which claims were pending, approved or settled
  • Settlement letters written manually for every claim
  • Team leaders fixing details at the end before the letter went out

What I built

  • A claim submission list and an approval tracker in SharePoint
  • Approval requests sent to managers in Outlook and Teams, with comments
  • A reject and resubmit path that keeps the manager’s feedback with the agent
  • A one-click flow that fills a letter template, converts it to PDF and emails the customer

How the new process flows

Step 1Agent submits the claim and gets a summary email
Step 2Manager approves in Outlook or Teams
Step 3Approved claim is logged automatically
Step 4Team leader sends the settlement letter in one click

Leave Planner PCC-MY

A leave request system that applies the team’s rules automatically and gives managers one view of leave across all teams. Built and launched in under three weeks.

The problem
